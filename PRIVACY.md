# Privacy Policy for Simple Workout Tracker

_Last updated: May 6, 2026_

Simple Workout Tracker ("the app") is built and maintained by Joshua Blaine as a personal project. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

## What data the app collects

**None.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## What data the app stores on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device, in its private application storage:

- Your workout sessions (date, duration, completion status)
- Sets you log (exercise, set number, weight, completion status)
- Workout reminder schedules you set
- Custom workout plans you import

This data never leaves your device. It is not synced to a cloud, sent to any server, or shared with any third party. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — used only to deliver workout reminders you have configured locally. No push notifications come from any server.
- **Exact alarms / boot completion** — used to schedule local workout reminders and re-register them after device reboot.

## Third-party services

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. There are no third-party services embedded in the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
